About

I'm a mechanical engineer based in Plainfield, Illinois, with experience spanning mechanical design, fluid systems, controls, embedded electronics, software development, and project management. My work focuses on taking engineering projects from initial concepts and analysis through design, implementation, testing, and validation.

I earned my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Kentucky in December 2025 and my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Southern Indiana in 2024.

For my master's thesis, supported by NASA Kentucky EPSCoR, I independently designed, built, and validated a 2D air-bearing satellite testbed for model predictive control using binary thrusters. The project included mechanical design and fabrication, pneumatic systems, cold-gas thrusters, custom ESP32-based electronics, embedded programming, control algorithms, simulation, and hardware validation. My undergraduate capstone was another independent project: the design of a lava rock crushing system.

My professional experience at Blue Lagoon Iceland began in 2017 as a maintenance technician. I later transitioned into project management, serving as a Project Manager from 2022 to 2025. Since April 2026, I have worked as a contract Project Engineer and Project Manager, with responsibilities covering piping networks, hydraulic calculations, CFD, pump selection, water and wastewater systems, and construction and renovation projects. I also served as interim Head of Maintenance, where I reduced the outstanding work-order backlog from 200 to 125.

Beyond mechanical engineering, I independently developed Grain, an AI-assisted maintenance and facility management platform designed around automated workflows, asset management, and human oversight of AI-driven actions. The project combines software engineering, system architecture, and practical maintenance operations experience.

Outside of engineering, I played NCAA Division I soccer at the University of Southern Indiana.

Engineering and validation: Technical results presented in this portfolio distinguish between physical testing, numerical simulation, and software-only validation. Performance claims are supported by documented results.
