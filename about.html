About

I’m a mechanical engineer in Plainfield, Illinois. I like the stretch from a sketch to a thing that moves: the mechanism, the loads, the controller, and the software that lets someone else run it.

Right now that is project and facilities work at Blue Lagoon Iceland, started alongside school in the U.S. The master’s at Kentucky was a planar satellite on air bearings. Before that, the undergraduate capstone was a lava-rock crusher and dryer. On my own time I am building a chess robot and a maintenance system called Grain.

If a number is on this site, it comes from a test, a thesis, or a saved simulation. Software checks are labeled as software checks. Hardware that is still on the bench is labeled that way.

    Controls and robotics

  2. Design

  4. Facilities

    Project systems

    MaintainX, Connecteam, contractor coordination, Microsoft 365, Power Automate, Copilot Studio.
