About

I’m a mechanical engineer in Plainfield, Illinois. I design and build complete systems from concept through validated hardware and software: machines, fluid and thermal plants, controls, and the software that runs them.

I earned an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Kentucky in December 2025 and a B.Sc. from the University of Southern Indiana in 2024. Since April 2026 I have been a Project Engineer and Project Manager (contractor) at Blue Lagoon Iceland.

Systems I’ve designed

  1. M.Sc. thesis2025

    Planar CubeSat testbed

    I was the sole designer and builder of this NASA-sponsored air-bearing testbed: eight binary cold-gas thrusters (2000 psi tanks regulated to 40 psi), hand-built ESP32 electronics, and a real-time mixed-integer (MIQP) MPC controller running at 16.7 Hz. A digital twin with shared controller code stayed within 2% of hardware thruster usage over full missions. The documented hardware run finished at 0.024 m position error and 0.51° attitude error.

  2. Blue LagoonFluid and thermal systems

    Geothermal spa systems

    I redesigned the seawater intake and heating system for the entire spa, isolating the supply from the adjacent geothermal plant and routing it through high-temperature flash heaters. That improved water-level, temperature, and silica control and reduced operating cost. I designed 1 km of wastewater piping — routing, backup routes, and pump selection — and a 550 m cold-water network with flow simulation, valve sizing, and pump specifications, and used CFD to correct poor water renewal in a recirculating cave pool.

  3. Software2026 – Present

    Grain

    I designed and built this facility-management platform solo, from architecture through deployment: isolated data per organization, server-enforced permissions, an audit trail, and an AI Manager that operates under human oversight. It has been running in production since November 2026.

  4. ProjectsControls and machines

    Controls and machine design

    I extended the satellite work to six-degree-of-freedom simulation, with thrusters for translation and reaction wheels for attitude. One nonlinear MPC framework swings up and balances one- to eight-link cart pendulums, real-time for one to five links. I designed a dual-arm chess robot — inverse kinematics, collision-aware trajectories, ESP32 firmware, and a link-length optimizer; the hardware build is still to follow. The undergraduate capstone was a lava-rock processing system, validated with SolidWorks force analysis and a 3D-printed scaled prototype.

I am also designing an AI project manager for Blue Lagoon maintenance that connects MaintainX with a Microsoft Copilot Studio workflow of specialized agents. That work is in progress.
