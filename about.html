About

I’m a mechanical engineer in Plainfield, Illinois, in the western Chicago suburbs. My work runs from mechanical design and analysis through hardware, controls, and software.

I earned an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Kentucky in December 2025 and a B.Sc. from the University of Southern Indiana in 2024. The Kentucky thesis, funded by NASA KY EPSCoR, was a simulation-to-hardware validation of model predictive control for a binary-thruster 2D CubeSat testbed on air bearings. I designed and built the entire system: the structure, pneumatics and cold-gas thrusters, hand-soldered ESP32 electronics, control software, and the simulation and digital twin. Faculty advisors provided guidance only. The undergraduate capstone, also a solo project, was Design of a Lava Rock Crushing System.

Since April 2026 I have been a Project Engineer and Project Manager (contractor) at Blue Lagoon Iceland, working on piping, CFD, hydraulic calculations, pump selection, wastewater and cold-water systems, and construction and renovation. I started there as a maintenance technician in 2017 and was a project manager from 2022 to 2025. I also served as interim head of maintenance, cutting the work-order backlog from 200 to 125. Separately, I built Grain from scratch: a facility-management platform with a built-in AI manager that operates under human oversight. At Southern Indiana I played NCAA Division I soccer.
