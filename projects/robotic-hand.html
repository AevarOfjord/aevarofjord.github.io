09 / Robotics · CAD

Exploring articulated hand mechanisms.

A mechanical design exploration of an articulated robotic hand, documented through successive CAD and printable-model iterations.

Scope
Finger, hand & forearm mechanisms
Status
Design exploration
Tools
SolidWorks · Assembly modeling · STL preparation

Design iterations

The project archive is organized into three revisions. It includes finger segments, thumb components, forearm structures, locking features, and complete or partial assemblies.

Mechanical work

SolidWorks parts and assemblies describe how the components fit together. STL exports carry the geometry into a 3D-printing workflow, providing a basis for comparing revisions and preparing individual parts.

  • Articulated finger and thumb geometry.
  • Hand and forearm assembly studies.
  • Print-oriented component exports across multiple revisions.

Project scope

This entry documents the mechanical design work. It does not claim a completed autonomous hand, a tested grasping benchmark, or a clinically validated prosthesis.
