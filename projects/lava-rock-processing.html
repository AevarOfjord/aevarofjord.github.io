05 / Mechanical · Bachelor’s project

From volcanic rock to a working system.

A bachelor’s capstone combining the design of lava-rock crushing and conveying machinery with analysis and physical prototype testing.

Scope
Machine design & prototype validation
Status
Completed capstone
Tools
SolidWorks · Force analysis · 3D printing
Read the capstone report ↗

The challenge

Develop machinery for processing volcanic lava rock into useful commercial materials. The system brings crushing mechanisms, material handling, structural design, and practical manufacturing constraints together.

Design and analysis

The project work includes jaw-crusher and hammer-mill studies, conveyor layouts, force calculations, shaft and structural analysis, and detailed SolidWorks parts and assemblies.

  • Compare mechanisms and translate requirements into a mechanical design.
  • Evaluate loads, component dimensions, material choices, and structural behavior.
  • Document concepts, requirements, risks, cost, and the project schedule.

Prototype and evidence

A scaled functional prototype was produced with 3D printing and tested using real material samples. The reports connect those tests to the design calculations and CAD work.

The prototype provides evidence at its tested scale; full-size industrial throughput and durability are separate engineering questions.
